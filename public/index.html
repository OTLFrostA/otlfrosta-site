All guides →
Is Gemini Exporter free?+

Yes. It is free and open source, with no subscription or exporter account required.

Can I export all my Gemini conversations?+

Yes, for the conversations accessible to your account. Sync All discovers your history; Export selected creates files. If the online list is incomplete, an official Takeout ZIP can supplement it.

Does automatic backup work after Chrome is closed?+

No. Live Save updates Markdown in an authorized local folder during active browsing.

Can I include images and attachments?+

Yes, when the original files remain accessible. Expired resources cannot be reconstructed. See sample exports.

Does it support ChatGPT, Claude or Grok?+

The published extension supports Gemini. Other platform integrations are experimental in source builds.
