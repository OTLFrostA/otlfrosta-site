BULK GEMINI EXPORT

How to export all your Gemini chats at once

Stop opening and downloading Gemini conversations one by one. Gemini Exporter finds the history your account exposes, then saves the chats you select as files on your own device.

Free and open source. Your conversation exports are processed in Chrome and saved to your own device.

STEP BY STEP

How to do it

Sync your accessible chat list, choose what to save, and start the export from the Workbench. A scan discovers conversations; it does not create backup files until you export them.

  1. STEP 01

    Connect your Gemini account

    Install Gemini Exporter, grant Gemini access under Site access, sign in to Gemini, and open the Workbench from the extension.

  2. STEP 02

    Click Sync All

    Select Sync All to discover the conversation history Gemini currently exposes. Review the list and wait for scanning to finish.

  3. STEP 03

    Choose chats, format and destination

    Select all conversations or only the ones you need. Choose Markdown, PDF, HTML, or JSON, then Browser download or Save to local folder.

  4. STEP 04

    Click Export selected

    Choose Export selected to create the files. Check each status for completion, changes or missing resources. Skip exported can avoid redoing unchanged, fully completed exports.

KNOW WHAT YOU'RE SAVING

What is actually saved in a bulk export?

Each selected Gemini conversation becomes its own document in the format you choose. For browser downloads, multiple output files are automatically packaged in a ZIP. Saving to an authorized local folder writes the documents directly, which usually works better for very large batches.

The Workbench remembers export status, helping you see which conversations were saved, which changed, and which need attention. Available images or files can be included when you enable attachments; expired or inaccessible resources cannot be reconstructed.

What to keep in mind

Limits worth knowing: A Gemini online scan can only discover conversations the source service still exposes. When an older or unusually large history is incomplete, import the original Google Takeout ZIP as a supplement. Some missing attachments may remain unavailable.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Answers before you export.

Based on the published Gemini Exporter workflow and its current limitations.

Complete user guide ↗
Is Sync All already a backup? +

No. Sync All updates the local conversation catalog. Export selected is the action that writes backup files to your chosen destination.

Can I export hundreds or thousands of chats? +

You can select large batches, but what an online scan finds depends on Gemini's accessible history. For very large collections, use Save to local folder and supplement the scan with Google Takeout when necessary.

How do I avoid exporting the same conversation twice? +

Enable Skip exported. The extension can skip a fully completed export of the matching format when the known source hasn't changed. Changed or incomplete conversations can be exported again.
